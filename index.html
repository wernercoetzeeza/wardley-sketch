Wardley Sketch

Created by Werner

A minimalist sketch tool for Wardley Maps — rough out a map on your phone, then export it to a fuller editor.

Wardley Mapping was created by Simon Wardley in 2005 and given freely to the community under a Creative Commons Share Alike licence. This app is only a sketchpad for a method that is entirely his.
blog.gardeviance.org ↗
The map syntax read and written here is the OnlineWardleyMaps (OWM) format, created and maintained by Damon Skelhorn.
